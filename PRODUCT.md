# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People following a prescribed knee rehabilitation plan at home, often checking the tracker during or immediately after exercise. They need a clear, low-friction way to follow exercise steps, track sets, monitor symptoms, and keep notes for a physical therapist.

## Product Purpose

Knee Rehab Session Tracker is a static, mobile-friendly rehabilitation checklist. It helps users complete daily knee exercises, track session progress, record notes, review completion history, and optionally sync authenticated progress with Supabase. Success means the user can complete the prescribed plan safely and consistently without the interface adding uncertainty or distraction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, clinical. The tone should feel supportive and matter-of-fact, with safety guidance clearly visible and no gamified pressure.

## Anti-references

Do not make the product feel like a diagnosis tool, generic medical advice generator, fitness challenge app, or decorative wellness landing page. Avoid vague encouragement, gamified streak pressure, hidden safety warnings, and visual treatments that make medically relevant checklist content harder to scan.

## Design Principles

1. Safety information stays visible and easy to distinguish from routine tracking.
2. The daily workflow is optimized for repeat use on a phone.
3. Progress feedback is useful but never pressures the user to ignore symptoms.
4. Controls use familiar product UI patterns and stable touch targets.
5. Medical content remains clearly bounded to the prescribed rehab plan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for text contrast, keyboard operation, focus visibility, form labels, and responsive behavior. Support reduced motion preferences, preserve readable text sizes on mobile, and avoid relying on color alone for state or safety distinctions.
